Privacy Policy

CarCareHub (“we”, “our” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we may collect, use, store and protect information when you contact us or use our automotive services.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on your interaction with us, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, vehicle make and model, service or parts enquiry details, appointment preferences and information you choose to provide.

2. How we use information

We may use the information you provide to respond to enquiries, arrange or manage service appointments, prepare estimates or invoices, provide vehicle service updates, respond to parts enquiries and improve our customer service.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information with service providers who help us operate our business, or where disclosure is required by applicable law. We aim to limit information shared to what is reasonably necessary.

4. Communications

If you provide your contact details, we may contact you about your enquiry, appointment, invoice or requested service updates. You may ask us to stop non-essential communications at any time.

5. Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information and retain it only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above or as required by law. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information we hold, subject to applicable legal requirements. For step-by-step instructions on submitting a data deletion request, please refer to our Data Deletion Instructions.
