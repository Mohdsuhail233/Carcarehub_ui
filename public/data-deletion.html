Data Deletion Instructions

At CarCareHub, you have the right to request the deletion of your personal and vehicle service data that we hold, subject to legal and accounting record-retention requirements.

Step 1: Send a Deletion Request

Send an email from your registered email address to carcarehub0@gmail.com with the subject line “Data Deletion Request”.

Step 2: Provide Identification Details

Please include your Full Name, Phone Number, and Vehicle Registration/Make & Model associated with your service account so we can accurately locate your records.

Step 3: Verification & Processing

Our team will verify your identity within 48 hours and process the deletion of your personal records from our active database. You will receive an email confirmation once completed.
